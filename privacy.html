Privacy Policy

YMF Spanish. Last updated: October 19, 2026

In short: YMF Spanish works without an account. Your progress is saved only on your own device. If you choose to subscribe to our emails, we keep your email address only to send you news from Your Mexican Friend. We never sell or share it.

Who we are

YMF Spanish is a free app with interactive Mexican Spanish exercises made by Your Mexican Friend (yourmexicanfriend.com).

What the app stores

To remember your progress, the app saves the following only on your device (browser storage):

  • Which exercises you marked as done
  • Your favorite exercises
  • The last exercise you opened
  • Copies of the exercises, so they work offline

This information never leaves your phone and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data deletes it.

Email updates (optional)

You can choose to give us your email address to receive new exercises and subscriber-only material. This is optional and the app works fully without it.

  • What we collect: only the email address you type.
  • Why: to send you news, new exercises and material from Your Mexican Friend.
  • Where it is stored: in a private Google Form and Google Sheet owned by Your Mexican Friend.
  • Unsubscribe or delete it: write to urmexicanfriend@gmail.com or reply to any of our emails, and we will delete your address.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or logins
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No access to your camera, contacts, location or files
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties

Third-party services

  • Google Forms — if you subscribe, your email address is sent to Google Forms. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — the app loads its typography from Google's servers. Google may receive basic technical information such as your IP address, as with any website. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — the app is hosted on GitHub, which may keep standard server logs. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Text-to-speech — some exercises read words aloud using your device's built-in voice. This happens on your device.
  • A few exercises and tools open online versions on carlosalbertoramirez.github.io, also hosted on GitHub Pages.

Children

The app is suitable for learners of all ages. Email updates are intended for people 13 and older.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Write to us at urmexicanfriend@gmail.com.
