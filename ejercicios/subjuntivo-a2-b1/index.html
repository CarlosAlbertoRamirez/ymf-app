Practice the difference without stress.

In this activity, students practice when to use the indicative, the subjunctive, and the infinitive. The questions are mixed: grammar knowledge, true/false, classification, choosing the correct conjugation, error correction, and mini dialogues.

45 questions Mixed and randomized each time.
Clear feedback Correct answers are marked correctly.
No time limit Students can think and review.
A2–B1 level Easier than a full advanced subjunctive test.
Quick reminder:
Indicative = facts, routines, certainty.
Subjunctive = wishes, doubt, emotion, recommendations, possibility.
Infinitive = unconjugated form, like hablar, comer, vivir.
