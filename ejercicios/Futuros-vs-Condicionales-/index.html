Futuro vs Condicional

Simple future · Ir a + infinitive · Conditional · Regular and irregular verbs · B1 · Mexican Spanish

🔵 Futuro — hablaré 🩷 Condicional — hablaría 🟢 Ir a + infinitivo — voy a hablar ⚡ Irregulares 🔀 Mezcla By Carlos & Denisse
🔵 Simple future
hablar-é / -ás / -á / -emos / -án
  • Predictions and future plans
  • Promises and commitments
  • Guessing / probability about the present
🩷 Simple conditional
hablar-ía / -ías / -ía / -íamos / -ían
  • Hypothetical or unreal situations
  • Polite requests and courtesy
  • Result clause in a second conditional
🟢 Near future — ir a + infinitivo
voy / vas / va / vamos / van + a + infinitivo
  • Plans and intentions
  • Upcoming or clearly imminent actions
  • Very common in spoken Spanish
