⚠️ Key contrasts:
Tengo calor = I feel hot (my body)  ·  Hace calor = It is hot (weather)
Tengo frío = I feel cold (my body)  ·  Hace frío = It is cold (weather)
Es aburrido = He is a boring person (always)  ·  Está aburrido = He is bored (right now)
Es bueno = He is a good person  ·  Está bueno = It tastes good / he looks good
Es difícil = It is difficult (always)  ·  El café está caliente = The coffee is hot (right now)
