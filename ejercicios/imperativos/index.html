B1-B2 · Imperativo positivo y negativo

Practice commands with tú, usted, ustedes and nosotros.

This activity focuses especially on tú commands, but also includes formal commands, plural commands and nosotros commands. It also practices pronoun placement in affirmative and negative commands.

Tú commands

  • Affirmative: habla, come, escribe
  • Irregular: ven, di, sal, haz, ten, ve, pon, sé
  • Negative: no hables, no comas, no vengas

Pronouns

  • Affirmative: attach → dámelo
  • Negative: before verb → no me lo des
  • léelo / no lo leas
  • cómprala / no la compres

Usted / ustedes

  • usted: hable, coma, escriba
  • negative: no hable, no coma
  • ustedes: hablen, coman, escriban
  • no hablen, no lleguen

Nosotros

  • Let's study: estudiemos
  • Let's go: vayamos
  • Let's sit: sentémonos
  • Let's not sit: no nos sentemos
